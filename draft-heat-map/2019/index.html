How it works

Scoring. Each player's average is adjusted for missed games: all absences (including bye rounds) are filled with the estimated replacement-level score for their position, so a high scorer who misses rounds is rated fairly rather than rewarded or harshly penalised.

Positional ranking. Adjusted averages are z-scored within each position group (top 40 DEF, 56 MID, 16 RUC, 40 FWD), measuring how far above or below the positional mean each player sits. Dual-position players take their best z-score.

Colour. Each pick is ranked within a forward window of 8 picks (the pick itself plus the next 7), clamped at the end of the draft so the window is always exactly 8 wide. Rank 1 = best value in that window; Rank 8 = weakest. Updates as the season progresses.
